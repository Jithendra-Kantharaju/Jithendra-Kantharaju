## Selected engineering

<img src="assets/section-accent.svg" width="96" height="8" alt="" />

Two projects across cloud infrastructure, operational reliability, and applied AI.

### 01 · LedgerGuard

*Document AI / Financial controls*

An accounts payable workflow that combines structured invoice extraction, deterministic validation, and authorized human review.

- **Approval controls:** Separates invoice extraction from authorization and keeps exceptions available for human review. Shadow mode recorded intended actions without posting QuickBooks bills.
- **Evaluation:** **0 wrong auto-approvals across 425 synthetic invoice tests.** All **225 of 225 problem cases** were held from auto-approval.
- **Test scope:** The frozen test split used offline rules and OCR extraction. Live LLM accuracy and real customer invoice performance were outside that evaluation.
- **Deployment:** Previously demonstrated on AWS ECS Fargate. The demo stack has been decommissioned.

[LedgerGuard repository](https://github.com/Jithendra-Kantharaju/ledgerguard)

### 02 · Enterprise CloudOps AI Platform

*Cloud platform / Agentic AIOps*

An eight-service platform on AWS EKS with infrastructure as code, GitOps delivery, a RAG shopping assistant, and an incident diagnosis agent.

- **Delivery:** Reduced deployment time from **45 to 12 minutes** with parallel builds and GitOps delivery.
- **Incident evaluation:** **93.8% classification accuracy (15 of 16 scenarios)** in offline tests with mocked tool outputs.
- **RAG evaluation:** **95.5% refusal accuracy (21 of 22 scored cases)** within a 26-case suite. The assistant incorrectly refused one return-policy question.
- **Deployment:** The repository documents the EKS deployment and a separate optional Bedrock and Lambda integration. The offline incident evaluation used mocked outputs.

[Enterprise CloudOps AI Platform repository](https://github.com/Jithendra-Kantharaju/enterprise-cloudops-ai-platform)
